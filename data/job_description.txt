Job Title: Data Analyst Intern

Company: GE HealthCare

About the Role:
We are seeking a motivated Data Analyst Intern to join our analytics team.
The intern will work on real healthcare datasets, build dashboards, and
support data-driven decision making across clinical and operational teams.

Responsibilities:
- Clean, transform, and analyze structured and unstructured healthcare data
- Build dashboards and visualizations using tools like Power BI, Tableau, or Python
- Write SQL queries to extract insights from relational databases
- Apply statistical methods to identify trends and anomalies in data
- Collaborate with cross-functional teams to translate business questions into data analysis
- Document findings clearly and present them to non-technical stakeholders

Required Skills:
- Proficiency in Python or R for data analysis
- Strong SQL skills
- Experience with data visualization tools (Power BI, Tableau, or Matplotlib/Seaborn)
- Understanding of statistics and data cleaning techniques
- Familiarity with Excel for quick analysis
- Strong attention to detail and communication skills

Preferred Qualifications:
- Coursework or projects in healthcare analytics or biomedical data
- Experience with pandas, NumPy, and Jupyter Notebooks
- Exposure to machine learning fundamentals
- Prior internship or academic project involving real-world datasets

Education:
- Currently pursuing a Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Statistics,
  Data Science, or a related field
- Minimum CGPA of 7.0/10 or equivalent
